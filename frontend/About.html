About Us

Travelog was founded on September 1st 2026 as a project from three computer science students attending the University of Southern Indiana. What started as a simple project quickly snowballed into a full website dedicated to providing the very best vacation experiences. Travelog now serves as the prime website for planning your dream vacation. Just as our company started from a passing idea, so too can the vacation you've always wanted!
